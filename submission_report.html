3. Compliance with Non-Negotiable Requirements
  • ❌ Absolutely No Cron: Scheduling relies entirely on BullMQ delayed jobs backed by Redis sorted sets (zset). Zero OS-level or node-cron libraries are used.
  • 🛡️ Server Restart Resilience: When the backend reboots, the reconcilePendingJobsOnStartup() engine scans all pending jobs in PostgreSQL/SQLite and synchronizes remaining delay offsets into BullMQ. Future jobs send on time without duplication.
  • ⏱️ Concurrency & Throttling: Workers run with configurable concurrency (WORKER_CONCURRENCY=5) and enforce a mandatory inter-email delay (DELAY_BETWEEN_EMAILS_MS) to simulate provider throttling.
  • 🚦 Zero-Drop Rate Limiter: Rate limits are calculated using atomic Redis keys (ratelimit:{sender}:{hourWindow}). Over-limit jobs are safely rescheduled to the start of the next hour window rather than dropped.
  • 🔔 Live Slack Integration: Hitting a sender's rate limit triggers a rich Block Kit notification to their connected Slack channel. Also includes a manual "Send Live Alert" test button in the UI.
  • 📁 CSV Lead Parsing: The Compose modal accepts CSV/text files, extracts valid email addresses via regex, and displays the exact lead count with batch scheduling.
